Our story

Nalss Baked It

Love in every layer.

Potchefstroom · Est. 2025

It all started with one birthday cake. Just me, a bowl, and so much love packed into every layer for someone special. You took a bite, you smiled, you ordered again.

From birthday candles, to graduation cheers, to "just because" surprises, every celebration became a reason to bake. And then came the Sunday desserts. The cupcake boxes that turn quiet weekends into sweet moments.

"Layers of love" is our new tagline because that's exactly how I bake: one layer at a time, with care in every swirl and a little heart on top, just for you.

Thank you. Ke a leboha to everyone who ordered that first birthday cake and stayed for every celebration after. You took Nalss Baked It from a 2025 kitchen dream to this new logo I'm proud to share.

Here's to more birthdays, milestones, Sunday treats, and many more layers of love.

Business policy

  1. A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to confirm booking.
  2. Full payment must be made before collection of your order.
  3. All orders must be placed at least 3 days in advance.
  4. Late orders will attract an additional charge.
  5. We do not offer delivery. Customers are required to collect their orders or Bolt/Uber send at own risk.
  6. Please note that inspiration pictures are for reference only. Final products may not look exactly the same due to design and decorating variations.
